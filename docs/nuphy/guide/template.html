Getting started

Three steps to typing on every device you own.

Turn it on

The mode switch on the back edge has three positions: Off, Wired (USB cable) and Wireless (Bluetooth or 2.4G dongle).

Pair a device

{{fn LNK_BLE1}} … {{key fn:LNK_BLE3}}
Bluetooth 1, 2, 3
{{fn LNK_RF}}
2.4G dongle

Tap to switch between devices; {{fn LINK_PREV}} jumps back to the previous one. Hold 3 s to pair a new one. For the dongle, hold {{fn LNK_RF}} first, then plug it in.

Pick Mac or Windows

Set the OS switch to Mac or Win. Each device remembers its own choice, so your Mac on BT 1 stays Mac and your PC on the dongle stays Windows. On the cable it detects the OS by itself.

{{fnm MACWIN_TG}}
Flip it without the switch

Reading the lights

Lights stay off unless something changes how your keys behave. Each one sits on the side of the key it belongs to: Caps on the left, Num Lock on the right. While one is on, the rest of that strip dims so it stands out over any light effect.

Left · how your keys behave

{{STRIP c fx*}}Caps Lock on. Breathing: Caps Word is on. {{STRIP fx r fx*}}Win / ⌘ key locked {{STRIP fx fx mg fx*}}Game mode on {{STRIP b.dim*}}Connecting: the whole strip breathes in the connection's colour, blue Bluetooth, green 2.4G, yellow wired. Keys you press now are sent once it connects. {{STRIP b*}}Fast blink: pairing. Solid for 2 s: connected. Its key ({{key fn:LNK_BLE1}} … {{key fn:LNK_RF}}) lights up too.

Right · numpad & power

{{STRIP w fx*}}Num Lock on {{STRIP _* y y y}}Battery, one LED per 20%: green over 80%, yellow, orange, red at 20% or less. Shown at power-on, when you plug in, and with {{fn BAT_SHOW}}. The top LED breathes while charging. {{STRIP fx* r}}Charge soon: the bottom LED pulses red below 20%. {{STRIP _* w.dim w w}}You changed a setting: white, lit LEDs show your choice, dim ones the other options. Sleep & battery

The keyboard sleeps when you stop typing. Any key wakes it.

{{fn SLEEP_MODE}}  Sleep mode

{{STRIP _* w w w}}Full saving (default). Lights off after 2 min, chip off 4 min later. The first key after a long break may take a second. {{STRIP _* w.dim w w}}Lights only. Lights off after 6 min; wakes faster. {{STRIP _* w.dim w.dim w}}Never. For the cable or a full battery.

{{fnm AWAKE_TG}}
Keep the computer awakeNo idle sleep or screen lock, per device. The keyboard doesn't sleep on it either.

On battery the backlight runs at half brightness and fades out after 30 s without typing; any key brings it back. Below 20% it switches off. No sleep while charging, on the cable, or in game mode.

Gaming

Faster scanning, no surprise menus, and snap tap for shooters.

{{fnm GAME_MODE}}
Game modeFastest key scanning, sleep off. Hold to reset its settings.

Lock the Win / ⌘ keySo it can't open menus mid-game.

Snap tapYou strafe with A and D. Normally, holding A and pressing D makes the game see both and your character stops. With snap tap the newest key wins, so you change direction instantly. Some competitive games ban it.
